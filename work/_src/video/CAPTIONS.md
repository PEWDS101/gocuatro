# Post copy for the five reels

Arabic first — it's the main reach language. A Kurdish line is included underneath
for the Duhok audience; drop it if a caption runs long.

**Before you post to TikTok or Instagram Reels:** mute the built-in track and pick a
trending sound in the app. The music baked into these files is licensed and safe, but
in-app sounds get materially more reach, and the platform can quietly suppress video
that uses outside audio. Keep the baked audio for **Facebook, WhatsApp Status, and the
website**, where there is no in-app music library.

## Posting order

Don't dump all five at once. One every two or three days, in this order — strongest
hook first, and the two with the most universal appeal (food, shopping) leading:

1. **TENÛR** — food performs best, and the dish-preview interaction is the most
   satisfying thing to watch
2. **ZERÎ** — the cart totalling up is instantly understandable
3. **HÊZ** — the live countdown is the "wait, that's real?" moment
4. **ZAGROS** — colour switching, strong with a male audience
5. **ZANA** — the drag slider is great but the niche is narrowest

Pin the best performer to your profile and put `gocuatro.com` in the bio link.

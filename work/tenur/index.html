Our story

One oven.Forty years.

My grandfather built this tenûr with his own hands, out of clay dug from the riverbank. He baked the first nan in it the winter my father was born.

Nothing about it has changed. Same clay, same wood, same fire lit before dawn. We still slap the dough to the wall by hand, still turn the kebab over coals, still send it out the moment it comes off. There is no second oven and there is no shortcut.

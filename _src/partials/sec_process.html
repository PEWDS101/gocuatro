Process

What happens after you message us.

No mystery, no drawn-out back and forth. Four steps from first message to live site.

  1. 01

    Talk

    We learn what the site has to do, who it's for, and what would make it worth the money. Costs you nothing.

    • You tell us the business, the pages and the languages you need
    • We come back with what it will cost and what is included
    • Scope and price are agreed before anything is built
  2. 02

    Design

    You see the layout and the look before a line of code is written, and we change it until you're happy.

    • You review the design and say what should change
    • Arabic and Kurdish are laid out right-to-left from the start, not bolted on
    • Nothing is coded until you have approved how it looks
  3. 03

    Build

    We hand-code it, test it on real phones and browsers, and tune it for speed and search engines.

    • Tested on phones and on desktop, not only in a design file
    • Every enquiry, WhatsApp, booking or order button is tried by hand
    • Pages are structured so search engines can read what each one is about
  4. 04

    Launch

    We put it live, hand over every file, and show you how to keep it running on your own.

    • The finished website files are handed over to you
    • No builder subscription, and nothing locked to us
    • We show you how to update it, or you can ask us to

What moves the number

Why two websites cost different amounts.

  • Number of pages One page reads differently from twelve, and each one has to be written and laid out.
  • Languages Arabic and Kurdish need right-to-left layout and their own typography, not a translated copy.
  • Content readiness Text and photos ready to go is the cheapest and fastest path. Writing and sourcing them takes longer.
  • Selling or accounts A cart, payments, stock or logins mean a back end, and a back end is real work.
  • Calculators, booking, ordering Anything a visitor interacts with is built and tested from scratch, so it behaves exactly as your business does.
  • Integrations Maps, analytics, a delivery service, a booking system — each one connects to something outside the site.

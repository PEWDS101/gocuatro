Process

What happens after you message us.

No mystery, no drawn-out back and forth. Four steps from first message to live site.

  1. 01

    Talk

    We learn what the site has to do, who it's for, and what would make it worth the money. Costs you nothing.

  2. 02

    Design

    You see the layout and the look before a line of code is written, and we change it until you're happy.

  3. 03

    Build

    We hand-code it, test it on real phones and browsers, and tune it for speed and search engines.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We put it live, hand over every file, and show you how to keep it running on your own.

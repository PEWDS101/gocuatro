Questions

Answered before you ask.

How much does a website cost?

Portfolio sites start at 75,000 IQD and digital menus at 105,000 IQD. Business websites start at 225,000 IQD, and most land somewhere from 225,000 to 750,000 IQD or more depending on pages, languages, content and features. You get one price for the whole project before any work starts.

What does the starting price actually include?

A complete, hand-built site for one purpose. For a restaurant that is a digital menu: your categories, dishes, prices, photos, contact details and a WhatsApp link that opens an order ready to send. For a freelancer it is a portfolio: your work, an About section, your services and direct contact links. Both are fast on a phone and both are yours to keep.

How long does a website take?

Most menus, portfolios and starter business websites are finished in 2 to 4 days, counted from the moment we have all the content and project details from you. Larger or custom websites get their own timeline with the quotation.

When does the 2–4 days start?

On the day we have everything we need from you — the text, the photos, the logo if you have one, and the answers about what the site must do. Not on the day you first message us. If content is still being written, the clock waits, and we will say so rather than let a date slip quietly.

Can you build in Arabic, Kurdish and English?

Yes, and in any combination of the three. Arabic and Kurdish are laid out right-to-left properly, with typography chosen for the script — not an English layout with the words swapped. A customer who reads Kurdish and a customer who reads English get the same site, each in their own language.

Do I own the finished website?

Yes, completely. Every file is handed over to you at launch. There is no builder subscription to keep paying and nothing is locked to us. Host it wherever you like, and hire anyone you like to change it later.

Can you redesign a website I already have?

Often, yes. Send the link and we will tell you honestly whether it is better value to improve what you have or to start again. Redesigns are quoted per project, because what is underneath varies enormously.

Can you add ordering, booking or a calculator?

Yes — WhatsApp ordering and quotation flows, appointment booking, product catalogues, online stores and custom calculators are all things we build. They are quoted per project, because the price follows what the thing has to do, not how many pages it sits on.

What do you need from me before starting?

What the business does and who it is for; the pages you want; the languages; your text and photos, or a note that you want help writing them; your logo if you have one; and the contact details and links the site should use. That is usually one message and one folder.

Do you work with clients outside Iraq?

Yes. Most of the work is remote and we are used to working across time zones. We write and talk in English, Arabic and Kurdish.

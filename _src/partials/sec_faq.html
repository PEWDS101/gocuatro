Questions

Answered before you ask.

How long does a website take?

Most sites go live two to four weeks after the first conversation. Online stores and multi-language builds take longer. You get a firm date before we start, not a guess afterwards.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and what has to work behind them. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll send one fixed price for the whole project — no hourly billing, no surprises at the end.

Do I own the website?

Yes, completely. You get every file, there's no builder subscription to keep paying, and nothing is locked to us. You can host it wherever you like and hire anyone you like to change it later.

Can you fix or update my existing site?

Often, yes. Send us the link and we'll tell you honestly whether it's better value to improve what you have or to start again.

Do you work with clients outside Iraq?

Most of our work is remote and we're used to working across time zones. We write and talk in English, Arabic and Kurdish.
